# GenPay launch video: plan

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 52s · **Tone:** polished startup launch · **Music:** original synth bed, 120 BPM (beat 0.5s, bar 2s), every cut on a bar line

**What it is:** GenPay is the closed-loop cashless wallet for General de Jesus College. Students pay stalls by QR, parents fund and supervise, merchants run a POS, and Finance keeps every GenCoin accounted for.
**Angle:** One wallet system with four portals. The tour follows money around campus: it gets paid at a stall, sent by a parent, rung up by a merchant and balanced by Finance.
**Hook:** A QR frame snaps shut, a check pops and the screen reads "Paid." Then: "No cash. No change."
**Visual identity:** Scan & Pay theme (theme.css). Forest `#0d2418` field, gold `#f0a500` accent, white 4px-radius cards, pill buttons, IBM Plex Mono numbers, Plus Jakarta Sans type, real gp_logo.png.
**Data:** All names, IDs and amounts on screen are fictional stand-ins (Juan Dela Cruz, 2025-00142 and so on). No real records are used.

## Storyboard

| # | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 | Hook | Scan frame closes on a QR → check → "Paid." / "−8 GC · Siomai Rice" → "No cash. No change." |
| 2 | 4–8 | Reveal | Logo seal + "GenPay" + real tagline "The Future of Campus Cashless Payments" · ₱10 = 1 GC |
| 3 | 8–12 | Roles | "One wallet. Four portals." Four cards (Student, Parent, Merchant, Finance) appear one by one |
| 4 | 12–20 | Student | "Scan. Pay. Send." The balance card counts up, transaction rows stream in, Send GenCoin types an ID → Sent, and the balance drops |
| 5 | 20–28 | Parent | "Allowance, limits, and a freeze switch." Allowance +20 GC sent, daily limit typed and saved, the Freeze Wallet toggle flips |
| 6 | 28–36 | Merchant | "A full POS for every stall." Items are tapped onto the ticket, a QR order code is shown "Awaiting payment", the ticket turns **Paid**, and a low-stock chip appears |
| 7 | 36–44 | Finance | "Every GenCoin, accounted for." The Wallet Pools stacked bar fills, the "All money accounted for" badge appears, and SHA-256 audit-chain blocks link up. A chip notes that large mints need the super-admin PIN |
| 8 | 44–48 | Stalls | "Vendors apply online." A10-stall map (rows A/B), one stall flips Available → Awarded |
| 9 | 48–52 | Outro | Logo + "Scan. Pay. Done." + "GenPay · General de Jesus College" |

Transitions: each scene's content fades out (0.35s) before the next fades in. The dip goes through the forest field, so two layouts never double-expose.
Sound: a soft kick on beats, an offbeat hat and a bass on the bar root (A minor → F → C → G). A pluck arpeggio comes in at scene 4. UI ticks are quantized to 8ths, a soft chime marks each scene start, and the last bar has a resolve and fade.
